💡 Collocation neden önemli? YDS kelime sorularının büyük kısmı bağlamsız ezberi değil, birlikte kullanılan kelimeleri sınar. "Make research" diyen biri konuşabilir ama YDS sorusu "conduct" ister. Eşdizimleri öğrenmek hem kelime hem gramer puanını yükseltir.

Fiil + İsim (Verb + Noun)

YDS'nin en çok sınadığı kategori. Hangi fiil hangi isimle gider?

EşdizimTürkçeYDS Örnek Cümle

conduct research / a study / an experiment / a surveyaraştırma / çalışma yürütmekScientists conducted extensive research into the effects of the compound. Sık

reach a conclusion / agreement / a goal / its peaksonuca / anlaşmaya varmak, zirveye ulaşmakAfter lengthy negotiations, the parties reached an agreement.
carry out research / an experiment / a task / a planaraştırma yürütmek, deney / görev yapmakThe team carried out a series of experiments over three years.
face challenges / difficulties / consequences / criticismzorluklarla / sonuçlarla yüzleşmekDeveloping nations face significant challenges in accessing clean water.
have an impact / an effect / implications / accessetkisi olmak, çıkarımı olmak, erişimi olmakDiet has a profound impact on cognitive development in early childhood. Sık
play a role / a part / a key rolerol oynamak, pay sahibi olmakGenetics plays a crucial role in determining susceptibility to disease.
achieve a goal / a result / success / a balancehedefe ulaşmak, başarı elde etmekFew countries have achieved a balance between economic growth and sustainability.
provide evidence / support / access / a solutionkanıt / destek sunmak, erişim / çözüm sağlamakThe findings provide strong evidence for the proposed mechanism.
undergo changes / treatment / a transformation / testingdeğişime / tedaviye uğramak, dönüşüm geçirmekThe region has undergone dramatic changes over the past century.
shed light on / insight intoışık tutmak, içgörü kazandırmakThis discovery sheds new light on the origins of the universe. Sık
address an issue / a problem / a need / concernsbir sorunu / ihtiyacı ele almakThe policy aims to address growing concerns about food security.
challenge assumptions / beliefs / the notionvarsayımları / inançları sorgulamakThis research challenges long-held assumptions about human evolution.

yield results / evidence / benefitssonuç / kanıt / yarar sağlamakYears of research finally yielded conclusive results.

Sıfat + İsim (Adjective + Noun)

EşdizimTürkçeYDS Örnek Cümle
significant impact / change / role / differenceönemli etki / değişim / rol / farkThe intervention had a significant impact on patient recovery rates. Sık
profound effect / impact / influence / changederin / köklü etki / değişimThe discovery had a profound effect on our understanding of genetics.
considerable amount / progress / attention / effortönemli miktarda / ilerleme / çabaA considerable amount of research has been devoted to this topic.
extensive research / damage / use / knowledgekapsamlı araştırma / bilgi, geniş çaplı hasarExtensive research has shown that sleep deprivation impairs performance. Sık
crucial role / factor / step / differencekritik / belirleyici rol / faktör / adımCommunication plays a crucial role in any organisation's success.
fundamental change / principle / difference / righttemel değişim / ilke / fark / hakThe industrial revolution brought about fundamental changes in society.
major challenge / factor / cause / concernbüyük / önemli zorluk / etken / endişeDeforestation remains a major cause of biodiversity loss. Sık
widespread use / concern / belief / damageyaygın kullanım / inanç / hasarThere is widespread concern about the safety of the new technology.
growing evidence / concern / awareness / demandartan kanıt / endişe / farkındalık / talepThere is growing evidence that diet affects mental health.
overwhelming evidence / majority / supportezici çoğunluk / kanıt, büyük destekOverwhelming evidence supports the link between smoking and cancer.
compelling evidence / argument / reasonikna edici kanıt / argüman / nedenThe study presents compelling evidence for the new hypothesis.
adverse effects / conditions / consequences / impactolumsuz etkileri / koşullar / sonuçlarThe drug had adverse effects on liver function. Sık
rapid growth / change / development / declinehızlı büyüme / değişim / gelişme / düşüşThe rapid growth of urban populations puts pressure on infrastructure.
scarce resources / data / evidencekıt kaynaklar / yetersiz veri / kanıtIn regions where resources are scarce, competition intensifies.
inherent risk / problem / advantage / limitationdoğasında var olan risk / sorun / avantajThere is an inherent risk in any experimental procedure.

İsim + Fiil / İsim Grupları (Noun Collocations)

Sık geçen isim grupları ve özne konumundaki isimlerle gelen fiiller.

EşdizimTürkçeYDS Örnek Cümle
evidence suggests / indicates / supportskanıtlar … gösteriyor / destekliyorEvidence suggests that early intervention significantly improves outcomes. Sık
research reveals / confirms / demonstrates / highlightsaraştırma … ortaya koyuyor / doğruluyorRecent research demonstrates a strong correlation between sleep and memory.
findings indicate / show / point to / challengebulgular … gösteriyor / işaret ediyorThe findings indicate that the treatment is effective in 80% of cases. Sık
population grows / declines / ages / shiftsnüfus artıyor / azalıyor / yaşlanıyorAs the global population grows, demand for food will intensify.
technology enables / facilitates / accelerates / disruptsteknoloji … mümkün kılıyor / hızlandırıyorDigital technology facilitates access to information on a global scale.
debate continues / surrounds / centres on / ragestartışma devam ediyor / odaklanıyorThe debate continues over the most effective approach to education reform.
concern arises / grows / mounts / remainsendişe doğuyor / artıyor / süruyorConcern mounts over the long-term effects of pesticide use.
policy aims to / seeks to / fails to / addressespolitika hedefliyor / başarısız oluyorThe new policy aims to reduce carbon emissions by 40% by 2035.
problem persists / stems from / worsens / is compoundedsorun devam ediyor / kaynaklanıyor / derinleşiyorThe problem persists despite years of government intervention.

on the basis oftemelinde / dayanarakDecisions should not be made on the basis of incomplete data.

attributed to-e atfedilen / bağlananThe decline was attributed to increased pollution levels.

subject to-e tabi / maruz kalanThe results are subject to further verification.

Zarf + Sıfat (Adverb + Adjective)

Akademik metinlerdeki güçlü vurgu zarfları. Eş anlamlı kelime sorularında çıkar.

EşdizimTürkçeYDS Örnek Cümle
widely accepted / recognised / used / regardedyaygın biçimde kabul edilen / tanınan / kullanılanIt is widely accepted that exercise improves mental health. Sık
closely linked / related / associated / examinedyakından bağlantılı / ilişkili / incelenenPoverty and poor health are closely linked phenomena.
largely due to / responsible for / unknown / overlookedbüyük ölçüde / çoğunluklaThe success was largely due to improved communication. Sık
increasingly important / common / difficult / evidentgiderek daha önemli / yaygın / zorIt is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore the effects of climate change.
highly effective / significant / unlikely / competitiveson derece etkili / önemli / rekabetçiThe treatment proved highly effective in clinical trials. Sık
particularly important / relevant / effective / vulnerableözellikle önemli / ilgili / etkinChildren are particularly vulnerable to the effects of air pollution.
strongly influenced / correlated / suggested / recommendedgüçlü biçimde etkilenen / önerilenBehaviour is strongly influenced by cultural norms.
potentially harmful / dangerous / significant / usefulpotansiyel olarak zararlı / tehlikeli / yararlıThe chemical is potentially harmful to aquatic life.
